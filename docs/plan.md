# Project Plan — Dao De Jing

## Project Name

Dao De Jing Learning Site (道德經)

---

# Vision

Create a clean, accessible website for reading and studying all 81 chapters of the Dao De Jing — one of the most influential texts in Chinese philosophy. The site serves readers who want to explore Laozi's teaching with Traditional Chinese explanations and audio support.

The site is a spoke within the **The Way Within (修身之道)** portal, sharing its visual identity and linking back to the hub.

---

# Target Audience

- Students of Chinese philosophy and Daoism
- Readers interested in classical Chinese thought and self-cultivation
- Mandarin learners exploring classical texts
- General readers seeking wisdom from the Daoist tradition

---

# Core Features

- Home page with a grid of all 81 chapters
- Individual chapter page for each of the 81 chapters
- Full original Chinese text per chapter
- Line-by-line Traditional Chinese explanations (逐句解釋)
- Chapter essence summary (章節精髓)
- Audio reading via Web Speech API (誦讀全文)
- Prev/Next chapter navigation on every chapter page
- Skip link for keyboard accessibility
- Portal bar linking back to The Way Within

---

# Design Principles

- Calm and scholarly visual tone
- Readable typography — Noto Serif SC for Chinese, Noto Sans SC for body
- Warm parchment background matching the portal
- Gold accents for interactive elements only
- Mobile-friendly responsive layout

---

# Non-Goals

- Not a social or community platform
- Not a translation site (English translation is not a priority)
- No database or server-side logic
- No user accounts or personalisation
